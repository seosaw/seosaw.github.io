    Field protocols & datasheets

    Standardised methods for African woodlands

    SEOSAW has developed a set of linked protocols to study the socio-ecology of African woodlands — designed to make data comparable across the network while remaining flexible enough to suit many different research objectives. We aim to:

    • Borrow from tried-and-tested methods used across tropical savannas and woodlands, rather than proposing novel approaches.
    • Enable data integration across the SEOSAW network and with other networks, including AfriTRON, ForestPlots.net and DRYFLOR.
    • Share field experience openly — what works, and what does not — so that mistakes are not repeated across the network.
    • Define a minimum measurement set for network integration without precluding additional measurements for specific projects.
    • Support long-term permanent plots designed to be remeasured approximately every 2–5 years.

    Reach out if you have questions about the protocols, or would like help preparing methods and datasheets for your project.
